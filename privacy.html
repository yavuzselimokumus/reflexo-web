Privacy Policy

Reflexo
Effective Date: 8 September 2026 · Previous version: 28 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Reflexo ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. We built Reflexo to respect your privacy: the app works fully offline and does not require an account.

01 Information We Collect

Reflexo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. We do not ask for your name, email, or any account credentials to play.

02 Data Stored on Your Device

Your game data is stored only on your device and never leaves it:

  • Your reflex test scores and personal bests
  • App preferences such as language and settings

This data is not accessible to us. Deleting the app removes it permanently.

03 Advertising & Third-Party Services

Reflexo shows advertisements delivered by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, when you allow it)
  • General device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions, clicks and invalid-activity signals

This information is used to deliver ads, cap how often the same ad is repeated, measure performance and detect invalid traffic. Ads are never displayed during a reflex test — they appear only on the home and settings screens and between sessions, so that what the app measures stays accurate.

Google’s handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can limit ad personalisation at any time: on Android via Settings → Privacy → Ads, on iOS via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Apart from advertising, Reflexo uses no analytics, attribution or tracking services. Your scores and settings are never uploaded.

04 Children's Privacy

Reflexo is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app displays advertising, we ask that children use it only with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

05 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If future versions of the app introduce features that use additional data, this policy will be updated accordingly before those features ship.
